Quickstart

From zero to your first scan in under a minute.

  1. Open t.me/FinnputerTradeBot and press Start.
  2. The bot creates a wallet for you. Its address is shown in the main menu.
  3. Paste any token address from Solana, Base, Ethereum, Robinhood Chain, Stable or Arc. The quick scan is back in seconds, the deep scan keeps running in the background.
  4. To trade, send a little SOL to your bot wallet. Then tap a buy button, or just write what you want, for example buy 0.05 SOL, take profit half at 2x, stop at minus 50.
You can export your private key at any time under Settings. Keep it safe and never share it.

How it works

Wallet memory

A ten minute old token has nothing to read. The wallets buying it usually do.

FINNPUTER keeps a record of what wallets did on earlier launches. Over 440,000 wallets are scored, and the number grows every day. When you scan a new token, that memory is matched against the wallets inside it.

What it recognises

  • Bundlers: wallets that bought the same launch together in the first blocks.
  • Snipers: wallets that buy launches within seconds, again and again.
  • Forwarded supply: wallets that pass tokens to fresh wallets to hide how much one group holds.
  • Serial dev wallets: creators with a history of launches that died.
  • Wallets with a real record: the ones worth following, used for signals.
Rated wallets and crews are never published. The memory is what makes the product work, so it stays private. You see what it finds, not the list itself.
How it works

Scans

Quick scan in seconds, deep scan in the background.

Quick scan

Contract checks, holders, liquidity, market cap and a safety score, ready before you decide.

Deep scan

Clusters, funding trace and forwarded supply. It takes about 90 seconds and updates the card when it is done, so it never holds up a buy.

Project research

One tap reads the project's website, terms and docs, checks domain age, and compares every claim against what is on chain. One research a day is free, Premium gets 10.

Reading the score

The score is about the token. Issuer controlled assets, for example tokenized stocks, are treated differently: an active mint or freeze authority is normal there and is shown as a note, not a failure.

How it works

Signals and the record

A signal says what wallets did. It does not say what a token will do.

Kinds of signals

SignalMeaning
🔥 Wallet signalA wallet we rate highly bought. Sent because of the wallet, not the token.
⚡ Rated walletsSeveral rated wallets bought the same token in a short window.
Meta rotationRated wallets moving from one kind of token into another. Premium only.

The live card

Every signal keeps updating: market cap now, the highest point after the signal, buys and sells in the last five minutes, liquidity and whether the dev still holds. Even when it is down, you see it.

How the record is counted

  • Every signal is counted, misses included.
  • We show the highest point after the signal, not a trade. When someone sells is not in our hands.
  • Market cap at the signal is recorded at the moment it is sent.

How it works

Trading

Say what you want. The bot scans first, then sets it up.

Your SOL balance buys on every supported chain: Solana, Base, Ethereum, Robinhood Chain, Stable and Arc. When a token sits on another chain, the bot bridges and swaps in one confirmation, so you never fund a second wallet.

Premium users, and $FINNPUTER holders at the time their signals arrive, can let the bot buy signals automatically, with a budget, a size per trade, chains, tiers and a market cap range. /why explains every skipped buy.

Theses

Why people are in, with receipts from the bot.

Anyone can post a thesis on a token: one message, up to 280 characters, no links. It is public with your name, and the bot adds the receipts on its own:

  • Market cap when you posted
  • Whether you bought in the bot before posting
  • Whether you sold after posting, and how long after
  • The highest point after the thesis, not a trade
  • Your record: how many of your theses saw 2x after posting

Share any thesis to X with one tap. The link opens it in the bot with all receipts. Amounts are never shown.

Help

FAQ

Is FINNPUTER free?

Scans, the web app and the extension are free. Trading has a flat fee. Premium unlocks live signals, meta rotation and auto-buy.

Do I need $FINNPUTER?

No. Holding it lowers your fee and gives you signals sooner, but every feature works without it.

Can I see which wallets you rate?

No. The rated wallets are the core of the product and stay private. You see what they do, not who they are.

Why did auto-buy not buy?

Send /why. It lists every recent signal and the exact reason it was skipped, for example the tier or market cap range you set.

Why does my portfolio show a price instead of a market cap?

The market cap needs the token's supply. If it cannot be read in time, the price is shown and the market cap appears on the next refresh.

Can I use my own wallet?

You can link a wallet to see its holdings. Trading runs from your bot wallet, which you can export at any time.

Common errors

If you seeWhat to do
Not enough SOLTop up your bot wallet. Keep a little SOL for network fees.
Slippage exceededThe price moved while the trade was sent. Try again or raise slippage in Settings.
No route foundThere is no pool that can fill this trade right now, often on very new or very thin tokens. Wait or use a smaller size.
Transaction expiredThe network was busy. Check your portfolio before retrying so you do not buy twice.
Hard fail on a scanThe scan found something that blocks a safe trade, for example a honeypot or active mint on a memecoin. Auto-buy will not buy it.
